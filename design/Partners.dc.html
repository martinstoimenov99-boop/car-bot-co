Partner questions

Is this an affiliate program?

Yes, it works like one: your own tracking link, a discount code and commissions on every dealership you bring. We call you a partner because you get real support and a long-term relationship, not just a link.

Do I need technical knowledge?

None. You never set anything up and never fix anything. We do the full setup, run the bot and handle every problem.

Who can I bring?

Car dealers and importers who buy used cars in Europe, from any country in the Balkans or Europe. They can talk to you in their own language.

How long do I earn from one dealership?

€150 at signup, then €50 each month the dealership pays, up to 12 months, plus a €50 bonus when they’re still with us at month 6. That’s up to €800.

What if the dealership cancels?

The monthly commission stops when they stop paying. That’s why a friendly check-in from you helps: happy dealers stay.

Am I responsible if something goes wrong?

No. Setup, fixes, support and every technical question are ours. If a dealer has a problem, you pass it to us.

How do I get paid?

Your earnings are tracked in your partner dashboard. If your dealership takes the yearly plan, you get a flat €800 once we receive their payment. For monthly plans we send the payout details in your welcome package.
