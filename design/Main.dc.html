Questions

Who does the seller think he’s talking to?

To you. The message goes out in your name, from your account. When he replies, you’re the one who calls.

I don’t speak German, Dutch or Italian. Is that a problem?

No. The first message goes out in the seller’s own language. After that most sellers are happy with a call, simple English, or a translator.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. We set everything up. You just use WhatsApp, like you already do.

Can I check messages before they go out?

Yes. Fully automatic, or one tap on WhatsApp to approve each car. Your choice, any time.

Do you negotiate or buy for me?

No. The bot gets you the first conversation. Price, payment, papers and transport stay with you.

Which cars can it look for?

Any make and model you buy, with your years, kilometres and budget. Damaged cars too, if that’s your business.

Why €250 a month? Alerts cost €30.

An alert tells you about a car. It doesn’t write to the seller, doesn’t write in the seller’s language and doesn’t work at 7 a.m. while you sleep. Your fee pays for the AI, the sender, the upkeep and a real person. One extra car a month pays it back many times.

Is it really AI?

Yes. A few AI helpers work in the background: one reads the listings, one checks the car against your rules, one writes the message in the seller’s language. The automation sends it. We watch over all of it.

Will sellers think it’s spam?

No. One polite, personal message per car, in proper German, never the same text twice. It reads like a serious buyer, because behind it there is one: you.

Is it legal and safe?

It’s built to follow the marketplace rules and EU data protection. Messages are honest and in your real name, and the bot stops if a platform asks for a check. If anything goes wrong, we fix it at our cost. More in the safety section above.

What if something breaks, or I get no messages?

Write to us on WhatsApp. We answer and start the fix within 24 hours, and it’s on us.

How fast is the setup, and where does it run?

24 to 48 hours after our call. On your computer, in our cloud or on our own servers, whichever we pick together. You never touch the tech.

What happens in the free test?

You send up to 3 mobile.de searches. We run them on our side and message sellers from our own account. If a seller answers, you get a WhatsApp from us and you decide if you take over. No card, no strings.

Do you take a cut of my deals?

Never. The price is fixed. Every euro of profit stays with you.

Can I cancel? Can I use Telegram?

Monthly plan: cancel any month. Yearly plan: you’ve paid for 12 months, with setup free. We start with WhatsApp and can set up Telegram too.
